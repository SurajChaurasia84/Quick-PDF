Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

Welcome to Quick PDF. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is handled, and how we keep your data secure while you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Access

Quick PDF is an offline-first utility application designed to read, scan, edit, and manage document files directly on your device. We operate with a strong commitment to privacy:

  • Local File and Storage Access: The app requests permission to access your device's external storage (such as the MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission). This is required strictly to scan, identify, display, open, and perform operations on PDF and Office documents (.docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .txt, etc.) on your device. All processing is executed locally. We do not upload or send your files to any remote server.
  • Camera Access: The application requires access to the device's camera for the document scanner feature. The images captured are processed locally to create PDFs and extract text (via local OCR text recognition). We do not store or transmit captured camera frames outside your device.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal identifiers, email addresses (except when you contact support), phone numbers, contacts, location, search history, or personal usage statistics.

2. Third-Party Services

Quick PDF is fully offline and does not integrate third-party trackers, advertisements, or analytics SDKs that collect or share your personal data. Any text recognition (OCR) or document viewing processing is performed directly on your local CPU/GPU using on-device models.

3. Data Retention and Security

Since all your files are stored locally on your device's filesystem (in your designated downloads folders, private sandbox, or documents directory), you retain full ownership and control over your files. The safety of your documents depends on the physical and access security of your device. Uninstalling the application will remove files stored inside the app's private sandbox folder.

4. Rationale for Requested Android Permissions

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Necessary for scanning and indexing files on your device to let you select, view, and organize PDFs and documents across different folders.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Necessary for capturing photos of documents, receipts, or notes to scan and merge them into PDF format.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
